This week we continue to develop the audio component of our prototype, having already finalized the sound output, with only the user audio input remaining to complete the audio component. We are also revisiting and refining our ideas around the physical design of the solution — while we had initially envisioned a glasses-based form factor, the weight of the required hardware has led us to explore new possibilities for how the device can be built. Alongside this, we begin ideating and preparing our communication materials for ElectroDay, working towards a compelling and polished presentation of our project.
