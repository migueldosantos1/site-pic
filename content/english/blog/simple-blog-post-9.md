This week marked the beginning of the prototype development phase. We retrieved the necessary materials to start building the prototype, successfully gathering all the required components except for the camera, which was unfortunately unavailable at the time. With the materials in hand, the team began dividing the development tasks according to the requirements list, and we will look into sourcing the camera as soon as possible. Also, on a very positive note, we finally secured a partnership — we held a meeting with the Associação Promotora do Ensino dos Cegos, which provided us with excellent insights about our project that will be invaluable for its development going forward.
