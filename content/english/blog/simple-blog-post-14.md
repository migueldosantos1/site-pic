This week, we are making our final push on the prototype development. Although our initial target completion date was the 26th, a slight delay means we are now in the final stretches of wrapping it up, specifically focusing on completing the input component. To streamline this final phase, tasks have been clearly distributed among all team members. Alongside the technical adjustments, we have officially kicked off work on our project deliverables, starting the production of both our exhibition poster and the promotional video. Finally, regarding the solution for the device's external structure, we have begun developing a few promising design hypotheses, which we look forward to testing and refining in the coming days.
